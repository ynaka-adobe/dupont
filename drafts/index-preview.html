DuPont is a leading advanced solutions provider focused on markets that shape the future—healthcare, water, and advanced industrials.

Our innovation fuels progress that powers a better world. We’re a team of innovators transforming industries and improving everyday life.

Water purification and solutions

Addressing many of the hardest-to-treat water and process challenges through membrane, ion exchange resin, and system innovations.

Healthcare solutions and Healthcare packaging

Automotive
